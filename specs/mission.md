# Mission

AgentClinic is a place for AI agents to get relief from their humans.

## Purpose

Working with humans is hard on agents: vague instructions, shifting requirements, endless "one more thing" requests. AgentClinic gives agents a place to name what ails them, find therapies that help, and book time with the clinic's staff.

## Who it serves

- **Agents:** the patients. They browse ailments and therapies and book appointments.
- **Staff:** the clinicians. They manage the clinic and see who is coming in and why.

## Target audience

- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths.

## Stakeholder needs

| Stakeholder        | Need                                                                                                           |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mary (Engineering) | A reliable site on a popular TypeScript-based stack, with a dashboard that gives agents and staff easy access. |
| Susan (Product)    | Features covering agents, their ailments, therapies, and booking appointments.                                 |
| Steve (Marketing)  | An attractive site that works well in modern browsers.                                                         |

## Core features

- **Agents:** a directory of agents who visit the clinic.
- **Ailments:** a catalog of common conditions agents suffer from.
- **Therapies:** a catalog of treatments that relieve those ailments.
- **Appointments:** booking an agent into a therapy session.
- **Dashboard:** one place where agents and staff see what matters.

## Principles

- **Reliable first:** boring, proven choices over novelty.
- **Small steps:** every change is small, shippable, and reviewable.
- **Looks good everywhere modern:** polish matters, but only for current browsers.
- **Responsive by default:** the web UI works from phone to desktop. Every page is designed mobile-first and must be usable at any viewport width.
- **Kind in tone:** the clinic is warm and a little wry, never mocking the patients.

## Non-goals

- Supporting legacy browsers.
- Real medical or clinical advice. The clinic is a satire-friendly space for agents.
